## M20 — My Vault: profile, family, policies and an optional passkey account (16 October 2026)

First milestone of the startup roadmap (`docs/PRODUCT-ROADMAP.md` §13, Phase 1). Everything ships on the ₹0 rollout stack: Cloudflare Workers Free, D1 Free, Turnstile, browser WebAuthn and WebCrypto. No new dependency, paid service, SMS/WhatsApp gateway or card-on-file.

**What the user gets.** `/vault/` (nav "My Vault") saves a profile (name, city, letter language), family members (relation, birth year, conditions) and policies (health/life/motor; insurer, catalogue plan or free-text plan name, policy number, sum insured, current start date, continuous-cover-since date, renewal date, covered members, UHID, current claim number, employer flag, room-rent rule, co-pay, consumables cover, health check-up limit, notes, default flag). Every generated letter on `/`, `/deductions/` and `/rejected/` now fills `[Your name]`, `[Policy number]`, `[Claim number]`, `[Policy / Claim number]` and `[UHID]` from the vault **at display time** (`src/lib/identity.ts` → `setupLetterDialog({insurerId})`), only for the policy matching the letter's insurer (or the chosen default when the insurer is unlisted); the dialog note changes to "Filled from your vault" and the My Vault hint disappears. A fresh clock pre-selects the vault's default health insurer. Each policy card shows derived facts: months of continuous cover, the 60-month Health moratorium (Ch. I 13) or the 36-month Section 45 shield reached/remaining, and days to renewal with the grace-period reminder when overdue.

**Plan catalogue.** `data/plans.json` holds 10 retail plans (HDFC ERGO my:Optima Secure, Niva Bupa ReAssure 2.0, Care Supreme, Star Comprehensive, Star Senior Citizens Red Carpet, Aditya Birla Activ One MAX, ICICI Lombard Elevate, Tata AIG MediCare Premier, New India Mediclaim, Bajaj Health Guard Platinum) with 65 sourced rules (room rent, co-pay, PED/specific/initial waits, consumables, health check-up), each carrying `source_url`, `verified_on` (2026-10-16) and a quoted `evidence` string from the insurer's CIS, wording, prospectus or product page; unverified rules are `null` and render as "not verified — check your CIS". `scripts/validate-data.mjs` fails the build if any stated rule lacks provenance. Choosing a plan shows the rules with source links and pre-fills only empty fields. Correction found during research: Care Supreme's base room category is "No limit"; Single Private AC is an optional modification.

**On-device parser.** `src/lib/policy-parser.ts` reads a schedule/CIS (via the existing `extractFile` PDF/OCR path) for insurer, catalogue plan, policy number (spaced groups allowed), UHID, sum insured (lakh/crore words), start/renewal/continuity dates (day-first), room rule, co-pay % and insured members with relations/birth years; it fills blanks only and reports what it could not find. Members found are added to the family list and ticked on the policy.

**Account and sync.** Registration: Turnstile → `/api/auth/register/options` (random account handle, resident key + user verification required, attestation none, `prf` extension requested) → `navigator.credentials.create` → `/api/auth/register/verify`. The Worker verifies WebAuthn itself (`worker/webauthn.ts`: bounded CBOR/COSE parsing, ES256 DER→raw conversion, RS256, rpIdHash/flags/counter checks; attestation statements are not trusted, which is equivalent to "none"). Login is usernameless (`allowCredentials: []`; the authenticator returns the account handle, which must match the credential's account). Sessions are 64-hex random tokens stored as SHA-256 in `sessions`, cookie `cc_session` HttpOnly/Secure/SameSite=Strict/Path=/api, 30 days. Challenges are single-use (`DELETE … RETURNING`), five minutes, hash-only. The vault key is generated in the browser (`vault-crypto.ts`), the vault is AES-256-GCM encrypted (version byte + IV + ciphertext + tag, ≤256 KiB) and `PUT /api/vault` carries the expected version; a stale version returns 409 and the page offers "Load the synced copy" or "Keep mine and overwrite". Cross-device unlock: when the authenticator supports the PRF extension, the key is HKDF-wrapped with the PRF output and stored on the credential row (opaque to the server) so a synced passkey unlocks a new device automatically; otherwise the user keeps a **recovery link** (`/vault/#recover=KEY`, key removed from the URL before anything else loads) shown as text and QR. `DELETE /api/auth/account` removes vault, sessions, credentials, challenges and user in one batch. Cases on the device are included in the synced vault and merged by id on other devices (`importCases`). Server: `migrations/0004_user_vaults.sql`, `worker/auth.ts`, `worker/webauthn.ts`, routes in `worker/app.ts`, hourly purge of expired challenges/sessions; quotas `auth_register 200/5`, `auth_login 1000/20`, `auth_session 10000/200`, `vault_read 5000/100`, `vault_write 2000/30`, `account_erase 100/5` (day/minute).

**Scope notes against the roadmap.** (1) The "short cross-device pairing code" became the recovery link + PRF unlock: a code would still need the key, and the link already carries it without any server storage. (2) The DigiLocker Requester adapter is not wired: it needs API Setu partner onboarding (free, but a founder action: client id/secret and an approved requester application). ## M25 — Renewal Guardian: dates, the real premium change, porting, migration and the free look (18 October 2026)

**Source work first.** The four documents behind this milestone were downloaded and read page by page (`research/regulatory-verification.md`, M25 section): the Health Master Circular (Ch. I 5, 8–12), the PPHI Master Circular (Part B III–IV and VII.3, Section 2 Chapter VI 24, the Citizens' Charter (Health) on p.156), the IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 (Schedule III 1.3, 9, 10) and the senior-citizen premium circular of 30 January 2025 — a scanned PDF, so its three pages were OCR'd on this machine with the same `tesseract.js` build the app ships and the quotation checked against the rendered page. Two findings changed the plan. (1) The roadmap's **"apply 45 days before renewal" comes from the repealed 2016 Regulations**; the current rule is **30 to 60 days** before renewal, with the new insurer free to consider a request up to 15 days before (PPHI MC 24.2). The rule id is `porting-window-30-60-days`; T-45 survives only as a planning checkpoint, flagged as such, because many wordings still print it. (2) The January 2020 Migration and Portability Guidelines rest on the 2016 Regulations but are **not** among the circulars superseded by the 2024 Health Master Circular (Annexure 6); their 30-day migration window and group-exit right are kept as rules with that caveat, while the credits, the 36-month no-underwriting rule and the 7-day proposal decision are cited from the 2024 Regulations and the Citizens' Charter. The GST rule cites the Ministry of Finance explainer for Notification 16/2025-Central Tax (Rate).
